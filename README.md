# Cylinder Designer

Design a hydraulic cylinder step by step — force, bore and rod, speed and flow, buckling, tube wall, cap and welds,
rod end, tie rods, cushions, ports and dynamics — with a Learn section covering everything about cylinders.
Companion to [Materials Atlas](https://harsh3261.github.io/materials-atlas/) and [SPC Tools](https://harsh3261.github.io/spc-tools/).

Reference calculations only: verify against your cylinder standard, suppliers' data and the customer specification.
